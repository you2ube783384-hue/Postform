    31→# 1. PROJECT MISSION
    32→Build a production-ready, responsive e-commerce website for POSTFORM, a fashion/streetwear resale business that acquires available clothing, footwear and accessories and resells them internationally.
    33→The primary objective is NOT to build a complicated enterprise commerce platform. The objective is to create a visually exceptional, memorable and highly usable storefront with a distinctive Neo-Brutalist / Editorial Fashion aesthetic, while keeping the business workflow intentionally simple.
    34→The website must feel like a real, premium streetwear brand—not a generic AI-generated template.
    35→The current visual reference is a product-page design with warm white background, strong black rectangular borders, bold typography, monospace/technical UI text, yellow primary actions, purple secondary accents, structured product imagery and a highly organized editorial layout. Use this reference as a design-language reference only. Do not copy its branding, exact layout, assets, text, or identity.

    61→# 3. CORE DESIGN DIRECTION
    62→Design the site as a refined Neo-Brutalist + Fashion Editorial commerce experience.
    63→Brutalism intensity: approximately 4–5/10. It should have personality and strong visual structure without becoming experimental to the point of harming usability.
    64→Important: Do NOT simply recreate the supplied reference screenshot. Evolve its visual language into a more polished POSTFORM identity.
    65→Design principles:
    66→• Warm milky white background rather than harsh pure white.
    67→• Strong black borders are a defining visual feature.
    68→• Use rectangular/structured components.
    69→• Corners should be sharp or use only very small radius values.
    70→• High contrast and strong hierarchy.
    71→• Large, confident typography.
    72→• Combine modern fashion/editorial typography with monospace/technical UI typography.
    73→• Use black as the dominant structural color.
    74→• Use yellow and purple as controlled accent colors.
    75→• Product photography should be visually dominant.
    76→• Use generous whitespace while maintaining a dense, intentional grid.
    77→• Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics.
    78→• Avoid rounded “app card” design.
    79→• Avoid excessive shadows.
    80→• Avoid gradients.
    81→• Avoid glassmorphism.
    82→• Avoid excessive animation.
    83→• Avoid unnecessary decorative effects.
    84→The site should communicate: “This is a cool, curated streetwear store that looks expensive and different.”

   780→# 42. ORDER EMAIL GENERATION — IMPLEMENTATION DETAIL
   781→Generate a mailto: URL on the client only after the checkout data is assembled.
   782→Required:
   783→mailto recipient:
   784→postformproducts@haren.uk
   785→Subject:
   786→POSTFORM Order #<short-id> — New Order
   787→Body:
   788→Plain-text, readable, line-broken order information.
   789→URL-encode subject and body correctly.
   790→Because mailto URLs have practical length limitations, keep the generated email concise enough for normal carts. If the implementation reaches a safe length threshold, provide a fallback action such as “Copy Order Details” rather than silently failing.
